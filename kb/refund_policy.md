# Refund Policy

## Eligibility Window
- Customers may request refunds within **30 days** of the original purchase date.
- After 30 days: refunds are not issued; service credits may be offered at discretion.

## Conditions for Refund Approval
Refunds are granted when:
- Service was unavailable for **more than 4 consecutive hours** due to platform fault
- **Billing errors** occurred (duplicate charges, incorrect plan pricing)
- **Duplicate charges** confirmed by billing team
- Service materially failed to perform as documented for **7+ consecutive days**

## Refund Exclusions
Refunds are NOT provided for:
- Partial month usage (e.g. cancelling mid-cycle)
- Feature dissatisfaction without documented service failure
- Change of business requirements or strategy
- Downgrade requests (downgrade takes effect next cycle; no refund for current cycle)
- Discounted or promotional pricing plans

## Refund Processing
- Approved refunds processed within **7 business days**
- Refunds issued to original payment method only
- Partial refunds issued as service credits when original payment method is unavailable

## Service Credits vs. Cash Refunds
- **Service Credits**: Applied to next invoice; preferred resolution for ongoing customers
- **Cash Refunds**: Issued only for billing errors or within the 30-day eligibility window
- Credits do not expire for active accounts

## Churn Retention Playbook
When a customer threatens to cancel or has expressed dissatisfaction:

### Tier 1 — Standard Accounts (< $500/month)
- Offer: 1-month service credit (10% of annual value)
- Escalate to: Customer Success team
- Response SLA: 24 hours

### Tier 2 — Professional Accounts ($500–$2,000/month)
- Offer: Up to 2 months service credit OR temporary plan upgrade at no cost
- Escalate to: Senior Customer Success Manager
- Response SLA: 4 hours

### Tier 3 — Enterprise / High-Value Accounts (> $2,000/month or VIP)
- Offer: Custom retention package (credits, dedicated support, feature roadmap access)
- Escalate to: VP of Customer Success + Account Executive
- Response SLA: 1 hour
- Executive outreach within 24 hours

### Churn Signals That Require Immediate Action
- Customer has sent 2+ unanswered emails
- Customer explicitly threatens public reviews (G2, Trustpilot, Twitter)
- Customer requests account deletion
- Sentiment deterioration: 3+ consecutive negative emails

### Karen W. / retail-co.com Scenario Reference
- Multiple unanswered emails constitute a **Tier 2+ escalation**
- Threats to post on G2/Trustpilot/Capterra require immediate Senior CSM outreach
- Recommended offer: Full refund for the disputed period + 1 month credit for service disruption
- DO NOT send generic auto-reply — requires personalised human response

## Cancellation Policy
- Customers may cancel at any time via the billing portal or by emailing billing@platform.com
- Cancellations take effect at end of current billing period
- Data retained for 90 days post-cancellation, then permanently deleted
- Data export available before deletion on request
