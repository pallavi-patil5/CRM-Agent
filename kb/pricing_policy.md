# Pricing Policy

## Plans & Pricing

### Standard Plan
- $99/user/month (billed monthly)
- $84/user/month (billed annually — 15% discount)
- Up to 25 users
- Includes: Core CRM, email integration, basic reporting

### Professional Plan
- $199/user/month (billed monthly)
- $169/user/month (billed annually)
- Up to 100 users
- Includes: All Standard features + advanced analytics, API access (1,000 req/min), priority support

### Enterprise Plan
- Custom pricing — contact sales
- Unlimited users
- Includes: All Professional features + dedicated CSM, custom SLA, SSO, data residency options, API access up to 10,000 req/min

## Non-Profit Discount
- Registered 501(c)(3) or equivalent non-profit organizations receive a **30% discount** on Standard and Professional plans.
- Proof of non-profit status required (IRS determination letter or equivalent).
- Non-profit discount cannot be combined with annual billing discount.
- To apply: email billing@platform.com with subject "Non-Profit Discount Request".

## Annual Billing Discount
- Annual subscriptions receive a **15% discount** off monthly pricing.
- Annual plans are invoiced upfront for 12 months.
- Cannot be combined with non-profit discount.

## Mid-Cycle Upgrades (Pro-Rata Billing)
- Upgrades take effect immediately.
- Charges are calculated on a **pro-rata basis** for the remaining days in the current billing cycle.
- Formula: (New Plan Price - Current Plan Price) × (Remaining Days / Total Days in Month)
- Example: Upgrading from Standard to Professional on Day 15 of a 30-day month = 50% of the price difference charged immediately.

## Adding Seats Mid-Cycle
- Additional seats added mid-cycle are billed pro-rata for the remainder of the cycle.
- Seats are available immediately upon payment.

## Downgrades
- Downgrade requests must be submitted before the next renewal date.
- Downgrades take effect at the **start of the next billing cycle** — no partial refunds for unused time on current plan.

## Enterprise Custom Pricing
- Minimum 100 seats for Enterprise pricing.
- Custom API rate limits negotiable.
- White-label and reseller arrangements available — contact partnerships@platform.com.
- RFP responses available within 5 business days.

## Academic & Educational Pricing
- Universities and accredited educational institutions may qualify for 40% educational discount.
- Available on Standard plan only.
- Contact edu@platform.com for details.
