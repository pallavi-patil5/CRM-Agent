# Compliance FAQ

## GDPR Compliance

### Data Subject Rights
Customers and end-users have the following rights under GDPR:
- **Article 15** — Right of Access: Export all data held about an individual
- **Article 16** — Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate personal data
- **Article 17** — Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Delete all personal data
- **Article 20** — Right to Data Portability: Receive personal data in machine-readable format

### GDPR Article 20 — Data Portability Requests
- Statutory response window: **30 days** from receipt of request
- Extension: Up to 90 days for complex requests (customer must be notified within 30 days)
- Data format: JSON or CSV export
- **This is a legal obligation — failure to comply within 30 days may result in regulatory action**
- All Article 20 requests must be flagged to the **Legal & Compliance team immediately**
- Auto-acknowledgement must be sent within 24 hours citing the 30-day statutory window
- Do NOT send a generic auto-reply — must reference the specific GDPR article

### GDPR Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- DPA available upon request for all paid customers
- Contact: dpa@platform.com
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) included for international data transfers

### Data Residency
- Default: EU data centers (Ireland, Frankfurt)
- US data residency available on Enterprise plan
- APAC data residency available on Enterprise plan (Singapore)

## HIPAA Compliance

### Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
- BAA available for healthcare customers on Professional and Enterprise plans
- Contact: compliance@platform.com to request BAA
- BAA covers: data processing, storage, transmission of Protected Health Information (PHI)

### Technical Safeguards
- Data encrypted at rest: AES-256
- Data encrypted in transit: TLS 1.3
- Access logs retained for 6 years (HIPAA requirement)
- Role-based access controls enforced

### HIPAA Covered Entities
- Platform qualifies as a Business Associate under HIPAA
- PHI must not be stored in free-text email fields without BAA in place
- Healthcare customers must sign BAA before using platform for clinical workflows

## Security Compliance

### Incident Response
- Security incidents reported within 72 hours to affected customers (GDPR Article 33 requirement)
- Ransomware/data breach threats: escalate to security@platform.com and legal@platform.com immediately
- Security events logged in audit trail for 12 months

### Data Retention
- Active customer data: retained for duration of contract + 90 days
- Deleted account data: purged within 90 days of account closure
- Backup retention: 30 days of daily backups
- Audit logs: retained for 7 years

## Regulatory Inquiries
- Regulatory or government inquiries: escalate to legal@platform.com immediately
- Do not respond to regulatory inquiries without legal team approval
- Document all regulatory communications in the compliance ticket system
